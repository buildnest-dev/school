Trust, security & control

Built around the idea that school data is private.

These describe what the platform is being designed to provide. They are product goals, not certifications or verified guarantees.

  • Design goal

    Tenant isolation

    Each school's data is intended to be separated from every other school's.

  • Design goal

    Role-based permissions

    People should see and do only what their role permits.

  • Design goal

    Controlled access

    Administrators decide who is invited, and with which access.

  • Design goal

    Historical records

    Closed academic years are meant to remain available for reference.

  • Design goal

    Auditability

    Important actions are intended to leave a reviewable trail.
